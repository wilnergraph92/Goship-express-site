# Business Profile Checklist: By Hand

The free route for the Basic version, and the route whenever the connection is not approved yet. The owner reads their own profile and tells Claude what they see. Claude writes the answers to `<work>/raw/by-hand-profile.md`, so the profile and reviews checks can use them.

Checked November 2026:
- Editing a profile: https://support.google.com/business/answer/3039617 . Go to https://business.google.com/ and click **Edit profile**. Google reviews changes before they show.
- Getting a review link: Google's help page "Create a Google link or QR code to request reviews", at https://support.google.com/business . On a computer: **Read reviews**, then **Get more reviews**, then **Copy**.
- Review rules: https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114 . No rewards for reviews, and do not ask only happy customers.
- The name rule: https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177 . The name must be the real-world name, with no extra search words.

For Claude: one question per message, and wait. Say where on the screen to look.

1. "Go to https://business.google.com/ signed in with the account that manages your business. Do you see your business with an **Edit profile** button?" If not, they may not have claimed it, or it is under another account. Find out which, and note it.
2. "Click **Edit profile**. What is your main category, and what other categories are listed?"
3. "Is there a description? Paste it here." (It is public text, so pasting is fine.)
4. "Are your hours filled in, and are they right?"
5. "What website link is set? Does it go to your home page or a specific page?"
6. "Are your services listed? How many?"
7. "Is the phone number the same as on your website?"
8. "How many photos are there, and when was the newest one added?"
9. "Click **Read reviews**. How many reviews do you have, what is the rating, and what is the date of the newest one? Do the last few have replies?"
10. "Search Google for your main service and your town on your phone. Which businesses show on the map, and how many reviews does each have?"

Write each answer down as they give it. Skip any they do not know: one line in the notes, then carry on.
