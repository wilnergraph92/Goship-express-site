About us

Reliable logistics and shipping solutions that save you time!

Goship Express is a company specializing in shipping, receiving and delivering packages between the United States and Haiti. We support our customers with simple, fast solutions tailored to their needs.

Our main goal is to become an essential partner for individuals and businesses looking for reliable, transparent and efficient service.

Why choose us?

A reliable network between the United States and Haiti.

Solutions for individuals and businesses alike.

Clear tracking so you stay informed at every step.

A strong commitment to efficiency and customer satisfaction.

Our mission

Providing reliable, fast and affordable logistics solutions

Our mission is to provide reliable, fast and affordable logistics solutions while simplifying exchanges between our customers, their suppliers and their families. Every package is handled with care to offer a clear and reassuring experience.
